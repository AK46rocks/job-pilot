# Writing rules (from Aditya)

## CVs
- One page for applications, two pages only for the master CV.
- No em dashes. Use commas instead of semicolons.
- Use the job description's exact keyword spelling (React.js vs React, Node.js, CI/CD, RESTful APIs). Add the common alternative form once in Skills.
- Fill leftover space at the bottom with extra DappLooker points.
- Only use facts in cv/master_cv.json. Never invent skills, tools, metrics or employers. List gaps for Aditya instead.
- Keep real job titles. Keep reverse chronological order.
- Mark the AI WhatsApp Shopping Assistant as "in development".

## Messages, short answers and "why us" questions
- No hyphens (-) and no apostrophes (').
- Humanised, simple language, with a few small natural grammar slips.
- Keep it short unless asked otherwise.

## Formal cover letters for upload
- Clean and professional, no deliberate mistakes unless asked.

## Roles to skip or flag
- Any security clearance (SC, DV, eDV) or 5-year UK residency requirement.
- Roles that cannot be done from the UK or need US work authorisation.
- Principal/Staff/Lead roles needing 6+ years.
